<h3>⚖️ Court-MOE</h3>
<strong>Legal AI · Natural Language Processing · Mixture of Experts</strong>

<br/><br/>

What if legal judgment prediction could benefit from specialized experts rather than relying on a single general-purpose model?

Court-MOE explores this idea for Indian court judgments through a mixture-of-experts architecture. It combines **LegalBERT-based representations, expert models, and a learned routing mechanism** to investigate how different specialists can contribute to a prediction.

The project brings together legal-domain language modeling, representation learning, and model routing in a challenging real-world domain.

<h3>🧬 Transpolymer-PS</h3>
<strong>AI for Materials Science · Transformers · Regression</strong>

<br/><br/>

Molecules are more than strings of characters. Their representations carry information that can help us reason about material properties.

Transpolymer-PS explores **transformer-based prediction of six polymer properties from SMILES molecular representations**. The workflow connects molecular tokenization and learned representations with downstream property prediction.

It's an exploration of how ideas developed in modern machine learning can be applied to a domain far beyond conventional text.
